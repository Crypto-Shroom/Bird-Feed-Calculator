# Vetch: Evidence Review

**Status:** Practical preparation guidance implemented. The calculator record now represents **feed-grade heat-treated common vetch**; nutrition values and profile targets are unchanged.

## Current app guidance

The app requires a feed-grade heat-treated common-vetch product and states that raw vetch seed must not be fed. It gives pigeon/chicken guidance for a mixed formula or balanced ration, and it retains a companion-bird warning not to add common vetch to parrot, African-grey, budgie, or canary seed mixes.

## Historical audit record

The `vetch` record says “Common vetch, high protein.” It is currently blocked by the audit processing filter. The record does not list cultivar, seed treatment, processing, or a target bird species.

## External evidence

University of Kentucky poultry guidance states that raw common vetch (*Vicia sativa*) should be limited to 5–10% of a poultry diet and that heat treatment increases possible inclusion. It identifies γ-glutamyl-β-cyanoalanine plus vicine and convicine as reasons overfeeding is harmful. [1]

The cited Feedipedia review reports cyanogenic amino acids and cyanogenic glycosides in *Vicia sativa* seed, explains that poultry are particularly sensitive, and advises very low untreated inclusion. It further notes cultivar and detoxification-process dependence. [2]

## Interpretation for this app

The evidence supports a stricter position than the generic amber processing message alone: **unidentified common-vetch seed should not be automatically eligible**. That conclusion is driven by a real cultivar, processing, and inclusion-risk distinction—not merely an audit preference. However, the reviewed sources describe controlled poultry-feed use rather than an absolute all-species “fatal at any amount” rule. No pigeon/parrot-specific automatic inclusion rule is documented here.

| Question | Evidence-based status |
|---|---|
| Is generic vetch appropriate for automatic formula selection? | **No.** |
| Is controlled poultry use documented? | **Yes, conditionally**; raw inclusion is low and processing can change limits. [1] [2] |
| Does this justify adding vetch to the existing hard raw-toxicity red list without approval? | **No.** A red rule would change current behaviour and requires product-owner approval. |
| Is a live rule change justified now? | **No.** |

## Implemented product decision

The product owner approved practical preparation guidance. The calculator record now means feed-grade heat-treated common vetch; it does not authorize raw or unidentified vetch seed. Its selected-bird message limits its use to the displayed pigeon/chicken context and keeps it out of companion-bird seed mixes.
