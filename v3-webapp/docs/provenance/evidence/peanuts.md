# Peanuts: Pigeon Feed-Safety Review

**Status:** Product-owner-approved source and treat guidance. Peanut nutrition values, eligibility, and formula targets are unchanged.

## Current app guidance

Peanuts remain eligible as a high-fat ingredient. The app now instructs users to choose fresh, plain, unsalted bird-feed peanuts from a reputable aflatoxin-controlled source, discard damaged, musty, or mouldy nuts, and treat peanuts as a high-fat treat rather than a staple. The optimizer is expected to balance peanut fat when grains, legumes, and other eligible inventory are available; constrained inventory remains visible through the calculated nutrition result.

## Evidence basis

Peanuts are a recognised substrate for aflatoxin-producing moulds, and aflatoxins can cause liver damage and death in animals. [1] A controlled domestic-pigeon study found hepatic lesions and mortality after repeated oral aflatoxin B1 exposure, demonstrating that pigeon feed hygiene matters. [2] Roasting can reduce aflatoxin, but it does not reliably eliminate it; a food-safety study describes aflatoxins as thermally stable and found only partial reduction with additive-free roasting. [3]
