# Preparation Guidance Sources

**Purpose:** Source record for the practical preparation messages added for chickpeas, adzuki beans, lupins, and common vetch. The messages distinguish required preparation from ingredient eligibility; they do not alter the stored nutrition values or profile targets.

| Ingredient | Implementation rule | Evidence basis |
|---|---|---|
| Chickpeas | Cook or use a properly processed product; give companion birds plain, soft portions and preserve chicken feed as a balanced-ration use. | University of Kentucky poultry guidance describes chickpea antinutritional factors and heat-treatment/inclusion effects. [1] |
| Adzuki beans | Soak and cook until soft before offering; do not present raw adzuki beans as ready-to-feed. | A 2024 processing study found soaking/germination reduced phytic acid, tannins, and trypsin-inhibitor activity in adzuki beans. [2] |
| Lupins | Use feed-grade low-alkaloid sweet lupins only; do not use bitter garden lupins. | The poultry-legume review distinguishes low-alkaloid sweet lupins from bitter high-alkaloid products. [3] |
| Common vetch | Use a feed-grade heat-treated product and restrict it to the species/product guidance displayed in the app. | University of Kentucky poultry guidance and Feedipedia both identify common-vetch antinutritional factors and describe heat treatment/low-inclusion poultry use. [4] [5] |
