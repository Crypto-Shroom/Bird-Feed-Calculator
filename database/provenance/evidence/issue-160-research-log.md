## First-pass key source inspection

1. **Hullár, Meleg, Fekete & Romvári (1999), “Studies on the energy content of pigeon feeds I.”** The direct study used ten adult male homing pigeons and fed individual feedstuffs, including sunflower and hemp, alone in **whole-grain form** ad libitum with water and grit. It establishes pigeon-specific ingestion/digestibility context for the exact whole dry seed forms, but does not justify an unrestricted or staple-diet use.
2. **Amer et al. (2023), “Modulation of Immunity … Origanum majorana in Domestic Pigeons’ Diet.”** This study used commercial *O. majorana* powder at 0.5% and 1% in a mashed basal diet of 30-day pigeon squabs for 45 days. Its plant is **sweet marjoram, not necessarily culinary oregano (*O. vulgare*)**, so it cannot be silently treated as direct evidence for the requested oregano form.
3. **Melbourne Bird Veterinary Clinic, “Diet for Pet Pigeons” (2023).** An exotics-vet practice lists safflower in a whole-grain mixture for pet pigeons and cautions against incomplete seed/grain-only diets.
