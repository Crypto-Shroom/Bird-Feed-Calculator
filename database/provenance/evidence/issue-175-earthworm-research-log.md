# Issue #175 — Earthworm research log

## Scope

The parent issue says “Worms,” but current `main` already covers `live yellow mealworm larvae` and `dried mealworms`. This sub-task therefore covers only the distinct form `commercially raised live earthworms (Eisenia fetida), clean, appropriately sized, and not wild-caught or compost-contaminated`. Parasitic worms, worming medicines, vermicompost, earthworm meal, dried earthworms, mealworms, and wild-caught worms remain excluded.

## Evidence interpretation

Bird Vet Melbourne establishes that earthworms can carry or transmit parasite eggs in bird disease cycles, so “clean commercial culture” is a necessary boundary but not a positive feeding approval. The 2024 broiler study is the only direct controlled earthworm-feeding evidence located and supports a limited chicken formulated-feed context at 1% inclusion for a defined broiler experiment. No direct exact-form outcome was established for pigeon, parrot, African Grey, budgie, or canary. Existing mealworm records are not reused as earthworm evidence.
