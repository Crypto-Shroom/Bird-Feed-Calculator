## Form boundaries

The review does not transfer conclusions to leaves, stems/canes, seeds as separated products, frozen, dried, freeze-dried, cooked, preserved, sweetened, flavoured, chocolate-coated, juice, jam, syrup, mouldy, or spoiled raspberry products. Those are separate forms and must not inherit fresh-flesh outcomes.

## Counter-review plan

Before a final ledger record is proposed, test at least these contrary interpretations:

1. Generic berry or fruit guidance may not identify raspberry, its fresh form, or the selected species.
2. A source that names raspberry may not support a portion, formula role, dry-mix use, nutritional value, or another species.
3. Consumer, community, retailer, or owner guidance may be weaker than veterinary, extension, or academic evidence and cannot be upgraded silently.
4. A source that discusses a processed raspberry product cannot establish the fresh plain flesh outcome.

## P6 — Counter-review

1. **Pigeon uncertainty remains material.** Direct pigeon/dove raspberry evidence was not found after first-pass and targeted searches. General berry or pet-bird material is not enough to convert that row to limited.
2. **Canary support is weaker than budgie and African Grey support.** Lafeber lists raspberry for a group including canaries, whereas VCA's canary page provides only general fresh-produce context and does not name raspberry. The canary row is therefore group-scoped and qualified, not species-specific.
3. **Commercial and consumer guidance does not create a formula claim.** Lafeber and Vet Verified identify fresh fruit as an addition, snack, or occasional treat; neither supports a nutrient value, batch formulation role, daily universal amount, or a processed raspberry form.
4. **Search snippets can be wrong.** A snippet appeared to claim VCA lists raspberry for canaries, but direct page review did not support that. The direct page finding governs the canonical record.
5. **Fresh form boundaries are essential.** No conclusion is transferred to leaves, stems/canes, frozen, dried, freeze-dried, cooked, preserved, sweetened, flavoured, chocolate-coated, juice, jam, syrup, mouldy, or spoiled raspberry products.
