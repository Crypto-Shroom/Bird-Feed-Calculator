## Counter-review and conclusion

The counter-review tested whether reports of chicken hunting small mice, plant-based or occasional-carrion context in some psittacine species, seasonal insect context for canaries, and invertebrate context for some Columbiformes could establish the submitted form. They cannot: live hunting, insects, carrion in unspecified psittacine species, and cooked meat are distinct from deliberate whole raw mouse/rodent prey. No direct scientific or veterinary source established this exact form for any of the six supported birds after the documented first pass and targeted follow-up.

The owner’s existing Issue #68 decision excludes whole vertebrate prey from the supported calculator birds, while recognizing that chickens may sometimes eat mice and that this is not a regular-diet instruction. The resulting six rows use a transparent `owner_approved_policy`/`avoid` boundary; they retain the direct-evidence limitation rather than presenting the policy as proof of toxicity or as a cross-species inference.
