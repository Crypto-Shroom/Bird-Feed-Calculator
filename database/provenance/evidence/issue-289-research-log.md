# Issue #289 research log: parrot, African Grey and chicken diet ranges

Research done 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-23. Sources are registered in `sources.json`. This log records provenance only; it changes no runtime value (a ledger record does not authorize a calculator change).

## Question
Which protein, fat and fibre ranges, and which diet shares, do authoritative sources support for parrots, African Greys and chickens, given that a grain/seed mix is only part of their diet?

## Sources consulted
Merck Veterinary Manual psittacine page and owner page; Brightsmith 2012; VCA African Grey handout; UC Davis diet-conversion handout; Oregon State PNW 477; Penn State, Kentucky/eXtension and Merck laying-hen pages; Hirano 2010, Harper & Skinner 1998, Péron & Grosset 2013 and Seemanthini & Jani 2020; abstracts of Ullrey 1991, Hess 2002, Hoyle & Garlich 1987, Bavelaar & Beynen 2004 and Voltura 2024; Gilardi & Toft 2012. Where only an abstract was read, the source record says so.

## Not consulted or not confirmed
- Koutsos, Matson & Klasing 2001 (Psittaciformes review): not consulted; the citation is known only from search results.
- University of Maryland Extension scratch-share figure (about 10%): not confirmed; not used.
- Bavelaar & Beynen 2004: abstract only, full text not consulted.
- A Spanish clinic quoted in a press article (60% seed): not consulted; contradicts every veterinary source; not used.
- Dutch (Wageningen, CVB tables) and German (LfL Bayern, Landwirtschaftskammer) hobby-chicken guidance: searched, no authoritative source found.
- Retailer and feed-manufacturer pages (Lafeber products, Harrison's, Purina, Versele-Laga, BayWa): not evidence.

## Findings (each limited to its stated bird)
| Question | Supported by | Not supported by any source read |
|---|---|---|
| Parrot/Grey diet share | Pellets 50-75% (UC Davis), 75-80% (VCA), 75% pellet + 25% produce met nearly all recommendations (Brightsmith, Amazons) | Exact seed share (sources disagree) |
| Grey protein, maintenance | 10-15% (Merck psittacines) | Breeding and moult numbers for Greys |
| Parrot protein | 12 / 16 / 24% low/mean/high (Brightsmith Table 5, Amazons, dry weight); 12% minimum and about 22% for breeding/moult (AAFCO and Ullrey, via Hirano 2010, second-hand, as-fed) | |
| Parrot/Grey fat | 5-12% (Merck); 4-7% (Brightsmith Table 5; its text says 10%) | One agreed figure |
| Parrot/Grey fibre | none | any recommendation (Brightsmith reports only measured 5.9-6.55%) |
| Chicken scratch share | 10-15% of daily intake (Oregon State) | Protein of scratch grain |
| Chicken layer protein | about 16% (Oregon State), 16-18% (Penn State), 12.5-22.5% by intake (NRC via Merck) | Maintenance (non-laying) and natural-moult protein |
| Chicken fat, fibre | none | any authoritative figure |
| Small psittacines/passerines (budgie, canary) | adult protein at least 10-14%, growing 15-20%, 25% or more harmful (Harper & Skinner); no demonstrated benefit of dietary fibre | |

Owner decision 2026-10-23: take Brightsmith 2012 as the leading source for the Parrot profile. It covers Amazon parrots only, so for African Grey the species-specific Merck figures take priority and Brightsmith is at most a `related_species` source.
