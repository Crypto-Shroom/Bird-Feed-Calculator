### Inspected budgerigar preparation source

ExoticDirect, “What can budgies eat?” (Dorothy Schwarz, 28 November 2019), https://exoticdirect.co.uk/news/what-can-budgies-eat/ , identifies a dedicated “Budgie legumes” section. It says peas and beans are suitable for budgies but **“do not ever feed raw”**, and explicitly lists **chickpeas, green peas, lentils (yellow, green, black—not split), mung beans, and yellow peas**. ### Inspected chicken raw-lentil source

Ciurescu, G., Vasilachi, A., Habeanu, M., & Dragomir, C. (2017). “Effects of dietary lentil seeds inclusion on performance, carcass characteristics and cecal pH of broiler chickens.” *Indian Journal of Animal Sciences*, 87(9), 1130–1134. DOI: https://doi.org/10.56093/ijans.v87i9.74327 . In 1,000 day-old Cobb 500 broilers, diets contained 200 or 400 g/kg **raw lentil seeds** of two cultivars. Broilers given raw lentils had comparable body weight, gain, intake, and feed-conversion ratio to controls; specified digestive-organ measures and cecal pH were not affected, with limited cultivar/inclusion interactions. The authors conclude lentil seeds can replace soybean meal at inclusion levels up to **200 g/kg**. This supports a chicken **limited** outcome for raw dried lentil seeds at defined formulated-diet inclusion, not unlimited free-choice or sole-diet feeding.

### Inspected chicken raw-pea source

Hejdysz, M., Kaczmarek, S.A., Adamski, M., & Rutkowski, A. (2017). “Influence of graded inclusion of raw and extruded pea (*Pisum sativum* L.) meal on the performance and nutrient digestibility of broiler chickens.” *Animal Feed Science and Technology*, 230, 114–125. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.anifeedsci.2017.05.016 . The study used 960 one-day-old male Ross 308 broilers and raw pea meal made by grinding seed, alongside extruded pea, at 100–500 g/kg diet. Extrusion improved performance and nutrient digestibility versus raw pea; increasing raw-pea inclusion negatively affected nutrient digestibility and AME, with the authors noting higher raw-pea concentrations had previously reduced performance. This supports a chicken **limited** outcome for the selected raw dried pea form at controlled formulated-diet inclusion, with a material processing/inclusion limitation rather than a free-choice approval.

### Inspected chicken chickpea, thyme, and parsley sources

In 200 Ross 308 male broilers, raw unprocessed chickpea seeds replaced 50% of soybean-meal protein in grower/finisher diets, comprising 31.0–35.0% of diet; final body weight, feed consumption, and feed conversion did not differ from control. ### Inspected chicken raw-mung source

Creswell, D.C. (1981). “Nutritional Evaluation of Mung Beans (*Phaseolus aureus*) for Young Broiler Chickens.” *Poultry Science*, 60(8), 1905–1909. DOI: https://doi.org/10.3382/ps.0601905 .
