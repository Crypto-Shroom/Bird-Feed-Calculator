### Inspected budgerigar preparation source

ExoticDirect, “What can budgies eat?” (Dorothy Schwarz, 28 November 2019), https://exoticdirect.co.uk/news/what-can-budgies-eat/ , identifies a dedicated “Budgie legumes” section. It says peas and beans are suitable for budgies but **“do not ever feed raw”**, and explicitly lists **chickpeas, green peas, lentils (yellow, green, black—not split), mung beans, and yellow peas**. ### Inspected chicken raw-lentil source

Ciurescu, G., Vasilachi, A., Habeanu, M., & Dragomir, C.
