# Issue #168 research log — raw white beans and culinary herbs

## Research protocol

**AS_OF:** 2026-08-21

**Scope:** Provenance-only review of six exact food forms across pigeon, parrot, African Grey, budgie, canary, and chicken. This log does not approve active data, formula use, inventory use, calculator behavior, warnings, or public copy.

**Form policy:** Each review applies only to its named food and form. Fresh/dried ordinary culinary herb form-continuity can be used only where an evidence source addresses ordinary household culinary use and does not materially distinguish the forms. It does not extend to oil, extract, tincture, or other concentrated preparations. The two bean records are raw and dried; cooking, sprouting, and commercial prepared products are separate forms.

**Source policy:** A source is registered only if it has a stable public locator, documented organization/author, clear scope, and stated limitations. Each external source is classified by the project schema’s tier; results from one species are never applied as proof for another. Where direct evidence cannot establish the exact bird/form outcome after the documented targeted second search, the row remains `unresolved`.

**Counter-review plan:** For every proposed preparation requirement, test whether a direct species/form source instead supports a limited raw-food context; for every proposed allowed/limited conclusion, test whether source form, dose, product type, or species scope is narrower than the candidate claim.

> **Second-search completion statement and inference boundary:** A documented targeted second search has now been run for every bird/form pair that remained inconclusive after the independent first pass. A generic bird result, a different botanical, a materially different preparation, essential-oil/tea/extract evidence, or an unverified retail/community claim must **not silently** become active suitability. Where exact direct evidence remains absent, the owner-authorized route is an explicitly labelled `related_species` inference: the review must identify the shared ingredient identity and relevant constituent or preparation boundary, explain why the exposure/form comparison applies to the named target bird, cite source and locator, and state what remains unproven. Such an inference stays bounded and cannot establish a dose, formula, complete ration, medical use, runtime approval, or a different preparation. If that standard is not met, the outcome remains `unresolved`.
