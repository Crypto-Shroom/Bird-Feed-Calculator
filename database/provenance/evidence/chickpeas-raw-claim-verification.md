# Verification of the Supplied Raw-Chickpea Pigeon Claim

**Review date:** 18 August 2026

## Claim assessed

> Raw chickpeas contain anti-nutrients, including lectins and enzyme inhibitors, that are toxic to birds; they cause severe digestive distress and prevent nutrient absorption. A related statement asserted that raw lentils should account for only 5–10% of a pigeon grain mix.

## Verification result

The supplied wording **cannot replace the pigeon `unresolved` record**. I could not identify an original source with a stable citation and pigeon-specific evidence for raw chickpeas, the asserted toxicity severity, or the 5–10% raw-lentil rule.

| Question | Evidence found | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Do raw chickpeas contain lectin activity and other anti-nutritional factors? | Food-science and broiler-poultry literature identifies measurable lectin activity and anti-nutritional factors in raw chickpeas. [1] [2] | **Supported as a general chickpea-processing concern**, not as a pigeon outcome. |
| Is raw chickpea toxicity to pigeons established? | No sourceable direct pigeon chickpea/raw-pulse study or veterinary statement was identified. The pigeon-specific pages found were either general diet guidance, uncited cautionary guidance, or inaccessible social content. | **Not established.** The pigeon row remains `unresolved`. |
| Does the evidence support the absolute phrase “toxic to birds”? | The controlled broiler study reports raw-chickpea inclusion in a balanced experimental ration and does not support a blanket toxicity statement for all birds. [2] | **Overbroad.** It must not be used as a cross-species or universal toxicity claim. |
| Is a 5–10% raw-lentil rule sourceable? | A commercial pigeon-lentil page presented a 5% example for *cooked* lentils but did not provide the stated raw-lentil limit or a citation trail. [3] | **Not verified.** It must not be added to the ledger. |

## Effect on the ledger

No calculator behavior, formula, food eligibility, preparation instruction, or visitor-visible copy changes follow from this check. The pigeon raw-dried-chickpea record remains `unresolved` pending direct pigeon evidence. The existing parrot, African Grey, budgie, canary, and chicken records are unaffected.
